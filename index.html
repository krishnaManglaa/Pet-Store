Curated Pet Care Tips 💡

Easy, bite-sized advice verified by professional community vets to keep your companions healthy.

Tick Protection

Indian summers and monsoons bring massive tick challenges. Regular brushing, timely vet-approved spot-ons, and checks after walks are essential.

Bathing Frequency

Unless advised, avoid frequent bathing. For double-coated breeds like Retrievers or Huskies, deep brushing works better to keep coats clean.

Safe Desi Snacks

Plain curd (dahi) is excellent for digestion. Watermelon (completely deseeded) and small cucumber slices offer outstanding hydration on warm days.

Recall Training

Always associate your pet's name with positive things. Never call them using their name when you intend to scold them—or they will learn to ignore you.
